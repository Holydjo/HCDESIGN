Studio profile

Warm, modern workplaces with a northern point of view.

HCDESIGN is a commercial interior design studio working across Canada. We plan and design offices, studios and shared spaces that feel calm on a Monday and still look right in ten years.

Every project starts with how a team really works. From there we draw the plan, choose honest materials, and stay on site until the last light is hung.

  • Workplace strategy
  • Interior architecture
  • Furniture & lighting
